## LidAngleSensor

`DuoHinge/Core/LidAngleSensor.swift` includes a modified integration of
[LidAngleSensor](https://github.com/samhenrigold/LidAngleSensor) by Sam Henri
Gold, licensed under Apache License 2.0. The full license text is included in
[Licenses/LidAngleSensor-Apache-2.0.txt](Licenses/LidAngleSensor-Apache-2.0.txt).

The integration adds callback-based angle delivery, app lifecycle handling, and
reporting-interval management.
